## Limits of this record

- One artifact, one recipe file, one hardware configuration. Any change to the
  YAML changes the artifact identity and needs a new record.
- The 24 GB checkpoint is not redistributed; the digests above are the only
  identity anchor.
- No speed claim: the published curve compares three recipes whose C1 values
  differ within the spread of a single 900 s observation, and whose C16
  difference moves together with the KV budget.
